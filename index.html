3D Bounding Box Prediction

Built an end-to-end PyTorch pipeline for predicting 3D bounding boxes from point-cloud data using a PointNet-style model, custom preprocessing, weighted geometry-aware losses, MLflow tracking, and Optuna tuning. Improved mean IoU from 0.38 to 0.60 through better loss design, yaw modeling, and training stability, then exported the model to ONNX for nearly 2× faster inference.
